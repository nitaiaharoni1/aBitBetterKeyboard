Conversational Hebrew prediction model
=====================================

This product contains a transformed model derived only from these two
downloadable text corpora from the Leipzig Corpora Collection:

1. heb_news_2020_1M
   https://downloads.wortschatz-leipzig.de/corpora/heb_news_2020_1M.tar.gz
   SHA-256 e81bb0b4f2f13bc343da8b732768582075257634600c359e55283774690b3213

2. heb_newscrawl_2011_1M
   https://downloads.wortschatz-leipzig.de/corpora/heb_newscrawl_2011_1M.tar.gz
   SHA-256 ad78abb765890d4055bd5105b6ae0471c45c76ad0b0fcb54b55a4d8e193bac7b

Source and attribution: Leipzig Corpora Collection, Leipzig University
https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/

Transformation:

The source sentence rows were normalized to Unicode NFC. Bidirectional control
characters and Hebrew niqqud were removed. Hebrew geresh, gershayim, maqaf, and
curly apostrophes were normalized to keyboard-typeable equivalents. Only
observed Hebrew surface tokens were retained. Exact unigram, bigram, and
trigram counts were collected without joining across rejected tokens or
sentence boundaries. The 20,000 highest-count surface forms were retained with
minimum unigram, bigram, and trigram counts of 2, 5, and 5. Each context was
capped at eight observed followers and packed into the AKN1 v1 binary format.
No clitic form or other word form was manufactured.
